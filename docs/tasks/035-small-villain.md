# Task 035: A small friendly-looking villain (the Bolt Bug)

Design: D-077. Read D-002, D-019, D-041, D-058, D-061, D-073 and D-077 first. Depends on Task 034 (Flash exists and the power rules are in one place); do it after 034.

## Goal
Give him a small "enemy" to beat for the first time: a little rogue gadget, the **Bolt Bug**, that wanders on the ground. It can never hurt him (D-002: no lose state). He beats it with his powers, it turns into coins, and he feels brave.

- Existing: `core/levels/BubbleField.ts` and `bubbles.ts` (a big bubble takes 3 shots and shrinks with each, D-058: reuse that hit and shrink pattern), `core/levels/shots.ts` (`shotHitsBubble`), `core/levels/mega.ts` (walking into things), `core/levels/format.ts` (`LEGEND`, entity extraction), `core/levels/LevelScene.ts`, `core/levels/placeholders.ts` (code-drawn textures), how `car-1`, `magnet-1`, `boots-1` and the Task 034 `flash-1` level were added to the map, the sticker rules in `core/rewards/stickers.ts`.

## In scope
- **The Bolt Bug** (new legend letter, check free letters in `LEGEND`; one new entity kind): a small round gadget (about 1 tile wide), original design drawn in code (a purple-grey body, two big round eyes, a tiny bolt-shaped antenna, little legs; it must NOT resemble any known franchise character, and no stomp-on-the-head mechanic: that reads as Mario, see D-055). It trundles back and forth over a patrol stretch of about 4 tiles on flat ground at a slow speed (about 40% of his walking speed), turning with a small hop, never leaves the ground, never goes near a gap (a level validator rule: the whole patrol stretch is flat and at least 3 columns from gaps, the start and the goal).
- **It never hurts:** if he touches it, he is bumped gently back about one tile with a soft "boing" and a small star puff, no penalty, no float-back, no lost coins, and the bug gives a tiny cartoon "hee" (tone recipe). Mashing must stay harmless. It never blocks him for good: the bump is a short slide and he can always go on (it must be impossible to get stuck between the bug and a wall or a big bubble; test this).
- **He beats it with what he already has, no new button:**
  - Power shots: 3 hits, the bug shrinks and wobbles a little with each (reuse the big-bubble pattern and `BUBBLE.bigScales` style numbers), then pops in a burst of stars.
  - Mega Robo walking into it, the car driving into it and Flash running into it: it pops at once (one touch) with a bigger burst.
  - Rocket boots and magnet change nothing for it.
- **Reward:** it pops into 5 coins that fly to him like bubble coins (counted for stars and `bestCoins` like grid coins, so level totals and the star thresholds include them), the pop uses a happy sound (a new tone recipe `bugPop`, higher than a bubble pop) and the first time he ever beats a bug he gets a sticker through the existing sticker rules (add one more rule and one more sticker only if the catalogue allows it cleanly; otherwise skip the sticker and say so in the Result).
- **A new level** `bug-1` "Bolt Bug" added to the map the same way as `flash-1`: flat-ish, Left/Right/Jump/Power, 2 bugs far apart (a patrol each), small bubbles and coins, no gaps near the bugs, one Flash or car orb placed so he can try the quick way to beat a bug, 1-star for finishing, stars 20/28 style. No demo. `"variety": false` unless the shuffle rules (Task 012, `variety.ts`) are taught to keep bugs and their patrol stretch clear, in which case say what you did.
- Not in the first play of Grass 1-7, not in Practice, **not in zen yet** (we want to see how he reacts in one level first).
- Level validator and tests: a bug needs a flat patrol stretch clear of gaps, blocks, pads and bubbles; coin positions never inside the patrol; the patrol never overlaps the start or goal columns; bot test that finishes `bug-1` with and without defeating the bugs.
- Docs: `docs/architecture/levels.md` (a "Bolt Bug" section with all numbers), `docs/PROGRESS.md` (roadmap row, a real-device checklist for Task 035: does he notice the bug, is he scared or amused, does he try to shoot it, does the bump annoy him, does 3 shots feel right), `docs/DECISIONS.md` (append the next free D-number (D-080 or later) with what you decided and any deviations), this file's Result. Test hook `?bugs=0` to hide bugs in `bug-1` (a quick way to compare), documented with the other hooks.
