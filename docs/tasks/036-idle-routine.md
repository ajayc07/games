# Task 036: Idle routine (wave, then push-ups, then a playful taunt)

Cosmetic only, a follow-up to Task 019 (D-081, D-083). Nothing may change gameplay, hit boxes, physics, scores or fairness. Everything works with both looks (robot and ball) and all eight colours. Read D-081 and D-083 first.

## Goal
Extend the idle wave into a short, funny routine while he stands still: **wave, then push-ups, then a taunt**, then rest and repeat. It should make a 3-year-old giggle and make the robot feel like a character.

## Read first

- Existing: `core/avatar/polish.ts` (`IDLE_WAVE`, `IdleWave`, tested in `polish.test.ts`), `core/avatar/clips.ts` (`waveClip`, `RobotPose`, `blendPose`), `RobotLook.ts` and `BallLook.ts` (wave handling and blend weight), `AvatarDriver.ts`, `core/avatar/types.ts`, `core/audio/steps.ts` and `recipes.ts` (tone recipes), the auto-pause rule (Task 027, D-070).

## In scope
- **A routine instead of a single wave** (generalise `IdleWave` into a small state machine, e.g. `IdleRoutine`, keeping the same entry rules): after 7 s without a touch while he stands free on the ground, run **wave (1.8 s) -> push-ups -> taunt**, then rest 5 s and start again at the wave. Same cancel rules as the wave: any touch, key, held button or anything that stops him standing free ends it at once and he goes back to the normal idle pose smoothly; never in the last 10 s before the auto-pause; only the real player does it (not the demo ghost, picker or Input Lab). It must not run in the car, as Mega Robo, with Flash, magnet or boots on, while flying or celebrating (as the wave today).
- **Push-ups (robot look):** he lowers to the ground and does 3 push-ups (about 0.6 s each, about 1.8 s in all) in a side-on pose, arms bending and straightening, body staying flat, then stands up. A quiet tap per rep (a new tone recipe `pushupTap`, soft, through the normal audio path so sound on/off, Volume and the voice limiter apply). **Ball look:** 3 slow squash-and-stretch pulses on the spot (no arms), same timing and tap.
- **The taunt (robot look):** a playful, friendly "come on, catch me!" move, never mean: he turns toward the camera, beckons twice with one hand (palm up, fingers curling), tilts his head and does a small cheeky shimmy with a quick wink of the eyes (use what the rig can do: if there is no eye wink, use a head tilt and antenna wiggle) for about 1.8 s. A short cheeky tone (`tauntHee`, a small two-note "hee-hee", through the normal audio path). **Ball look:** two quick rolls left and right (about half a tile of rocking, no real movement of the body) and a happy hop. No text, no mean faces, nothing scary.
- All three moves blend in and out with the existing pose-blend weights, like the wave; no pops. The routine totals roughly 7 s of motion; check it fits comfortably before the auto-pause hint and never delays or changes it.
- Keep each move's numbers in one constants object (`IDLE_ROUTINE`: durations, reps, rest) and the state machine pure and unit tested: order wave -> push-ups -> taunt -> rest -> wave; cancel at each stage; no start when not free; no start before the auto-pause window; pose clips return poses within the same limits as the existing ones (no NaN, a valid rig value at t = 0, mid, end).
