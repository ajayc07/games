# Task 038: Cave enemies, the Rock Beetle and the Glow Bat

Design: D-085. Read D-002, D-080, D-082 and D-085 first. Depends on 037.

## Goal
Two new gentle enemies, a step up from the Bolt Bug (D-080) but still never able to hurt him: a tougher ground enemy and a flying one, each introduced in its own level.

## In scope
- **Reuse the Bug machinery** (`bugs.ts`, `BugField.ts`, validator, sticker rules). Generalise it to enemies by kind with per-kind data (hits, speed, patrol, size, coins, sounds); do not copy code. The Bolt Bug's numbers and behaviour must not change.
- **Rock Beetle** (ground, "a bit more powerful than the bug"): rounded grey-blue rock-shell beetle, about 1.4 tiles wide, patrol 6 tiles at 55% of his walk, **5 shots** (cracks show on the shell and it shrinks a little per hit), pops into **8 coins**. It peeks (head out, 1 s) at each end of its patrol. A touch is the same gentle bump (slide back, boing, tiny hee, 800 ms grace). One touch as Mega, car or Flash pops it. No stomp.
- **Glow Bat** (flying): small round bat with a glowing belly, slow horizontal sine patrol 4 tiles wide, about one and a half tiles above the ground, so he walks under it, jumps into its bump or shoots it. **3 shots**, pops into **6 coins**. Same gentle bump. Never dives, never chases. Hittable while he stands and while he jumps (test reach from both muzzle heights; hit circle stays full size).
- **Validator:** beetle needs a flat 6-tile patrol plus 3 tiles each side clear of gaps, blocks, pads, orbs, start and goal; bat needs 4 tiles of clear ground below and no block within its height; coins and bubbles never inside a patrol; enemies at least 6 tiles apart.
- **Levels:** `cave-2` "Rock Beetle" (2 beetles, a Flash or car orb, bubbles, about 85 tiles) and `cave-3` "Glow Bat" (3 bats and 1 beetle, about 90 tiles). Enemy coins count for stars (as D-080). No demos.
- **Stickers:** first beetle and first bat beaten each give a sticker if the catalogue allows it cleanly (saved flags kept on reset), else say so.
- **Sounds** as tone recipes (`beetleHit`, `beetlePop`, `batFlap` quiet, `batPop`) through the normal audio path.
- Hook `?enemies=0` hides all Cave enemies. Not in zen or Practice.
- Docs: `levels.md` ("Enemies" section with all numbers), PROGRESS (roadmap row, checklist), Result.
- One commit: `task-038: rock beetle, glow bat, cave-2 and cave-3`.

## Out of scope
Damage, health, lives, scary looks, dive or chase behaviour, enemy projectiles, the boss, zen, new buttons.

## Acceptance criteria
- typecheck, tests, build, banned-names pass; no image files; Bolt Bug tests unchanged and green.
- Bot finishes `cave-2` and `cave-3` ignoring enemies, shooting them, and using an orb; he can never get stuck (extend the 1-D bump test to both patrols).
- Screenshots at 844x390 and 1280x800 (`_sources/review/038/`): each enemy idle, after hits, popping into coins, bumping; clearly different from a bubble, a coin and the Bolt Bug; cute-grumpy, never scary.

## Result
- What changed: Two new gentle enemies on the Bolt Bug machinery, which is now generic by kind (`levels/enemies.ts`, `patrol.ts`, `EnemyField.ts`; the Bolt Bug's numbers, pose and tests are unchanged and a test pins its pose to the old one). **Rock Beetle** (`k`): a rounded grey-blue rock shell with a feeler head (cute-grumpy, about 1.4 tiles wide), patrol 6 tiles at 55% of his walk, head out for 1 s at each end, 5 shots (a crack on the shell and a slight shrink per hit), 8 coins. **Glow Bat** (`w`): a small purple bat with a glowing belly and flapping wings, a slow 4-tile sine 1.5 tiles above the ground, 3 shots, 6 coins; a standing robot walks under it, a jump into it bumps, a standing shot reaches it. Same gentle bump (slide back, boing, tiny hee, 800 ms grace), no stomp, a touch as Mega, car or Flash pops either at once. Validator rules for both, `LEGEND` `k` and `w`. Levels `cave-2` "Rock Beetle" (85 tiles, a car orb, 2 beetles, 7 bubbles, a gap) and `cave-3` "Glow Bat" (90 tiles, 3 bats and a beetle, 9 bubbles, two gaps, no orb so the Mega bar fills); both added to the Cave world (so the Cave map has three dots). Enemy coins count for the stars. Stickers for the first beetle and the first bat (saved flag `enemiesBeaten`, kept on a reset). Sounds `beetleHit`, `beetlePop`, `batFlap` (quiet), `batPop`. `?enemies=0` hides them; none in zen or Practice.
- Files touched: new `core/levels/{enemies,patrol}.ts`, `EnemyField.ts` (renamed from `BugField.ts`), `enemies.test.ts`; changed `core/levels/{bugs,format,grid,placeholders,LevelScene}.ts`, `core/audio/recipes.ts`, `core/rewards/stickers.ts`, `core/storage/{progress,store}.ts` (+ `progress.test.ts`), `universes/robo/{worlds,manifest}.ts`, new `levels/cave-2.json` and `cave-3.json`, `levels/levels.test.ts`; docs `PROGRESS.md`, `architecture/levels.md`, this file.
- Deviations from plan (and why): (1) The bat's picture and sine follow the task, but its hit and touch circle uses the middle of its cell (the 8 px bob only lifts the picture): the task wants "walks under it" and "hittable standing" together, which needs a generous hit circle (x1.9, about 51 px) and a small touch circle (x0.4); at the middle height a standing robot (84 px) is 1 px under the touch circle, and the lowest standing muzzle (32 px) is 1 px inside the hit circle, so both numbers are tight (tests check every pose). (2) The bat's validator rule is that its cell is exactly two rows above flat ground (that is what makes it 1.5 tiles up), plus flat ground one tile beyond its 2-tile sine. (3) `cave-2` uses a car orb (the task said "Flash or car"); the car (about 43 tiles) is still on at the first beetle and over before the second, so one beetle is popped by touch and the other is shot or passed. (4) `cave-3` has no orb, so the Mega bar exists (it fills at 70% of the coins). (5) The map's Cave now has three open dots at once (the usual three-open-at-a-time rule); the unlock chain inside the Cave was not asked for. (6) Where the task says "5 shots / 3 shots" the pictures shrink to 0.76 and 0.7; the beetle has a feeler head so it does not read as a turtle (a shell and a head under it looked too much like a certain franchise turtle). (7) `bugs.ts` kept its exports; `BugField.ts` became `EnemyField.ts`.
- How verified: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` (46 files, 583 tests: the Bolt Bug tests unchanged and green, 24 new enemy tests, the Cave block extended; the banned-names scan passes; no image files) and `npm run build`. Tests: reach from both standing muzzle heights for every picture size, a standing robot under the bat at every pose, the validator for both kinds and the 6-column spacing, the 1-D bump walk-through of the real beetle and bug patrols (right and left, with a wall behind him), a scripted bot on `cave-2` and `cave-3` as written and on 200 grids each with the rainbow bubble. In Chromium (Playwright, headless software GL) at 844x390 and 1280x800: both enemies idle, the beetle's cracks after 2 and 4 hits, the beetle and bat popping into coins (the first-beetle and first-bat stickers appeared), the beetle bump, walking under a bat without a bump, a jump near a bat, the car popping the first beetle on touch, `?enemies=0` (no enemies, 49 coins in cave-3), and the Cave map with three dots; no console errors. Sheets in `_sources/review/038/` (not committed).
- Open issues / risks: Not tried on a tablet or with him (is he scared or amused, 5 shots for the beetle, 3 for the bat, can he shoot the bat, does the beetle's peek read). At 1280x800 a bat 3-5 tiles ahead can sit behind the Power button (the old accepted quirk for items at the bottom right); at 844x390 it is clear. The bat's hit and touch numbers are tight (see 1). Headless fps is unmeasurable; the sounds were not heard by ear. The bump frame at 844x390 is small because the headless run is slow, and the enemy pop frames were taken a little late (the sticker pop-up covers the middle of some of them). No enemy has an intro (sticker aside).
