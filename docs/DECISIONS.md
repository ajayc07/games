## Product and child-first rules
- **D-001** Platformer with a control ladder, one new control per level. Now: Grass 1 tap anywhere to jump; Grass 2 big Jump button (auto-run); Grass 3 Left/Right; Grass 4 Power (shoot bubbles); Grass 5 move + jump over gaps; Grass 6 all four together (D-037, D-041, D-043). Free-play Combo Practice for combos (D-049).
- **D-002** No failure states, no lives, timers or game-over, no text needed to play (the parent view may use text, D-047). Mashing must be harmless.
- **D-019** A fall is never a failure: soft whoosh, a small cloud floats him back to the last safe ground (at least 3 tiles behind the gap, preferring ground on both sides), no penalty to stars. Task 008: the float-back puts him on the side he came from when he fell walking left.
- **D-020** Stars: finishing = 1; 2 and 3 come from coin thresholds declared per level.
- **D-003 / D-036** World map is the default start screen: level dots on a winding path, locked dots under clouds, 3 star slots per dot, the robot on the current dot (the first open level not yet cleared, D-040), teaser signposts for later worlds (cave, sky, castle). Unlock: level i is open if `i < clearedCount + openAtATime`; `openAtATime` is 3 (D-037); cleared = at least 1 star; optional `finalLevelStarsRequired` per world (unused); `?unlock=all`. In-level exit = a small house icon held 1 s (ring); a tap only wobbles it.
- **D-025** Win screen: Replay, Next (only when the next level exists and is open), Home (the map).
- **D-016** No Claude attribution in commits or PRs (no `Co-Authored-By`, `Claude-Session`, "Generated with"); commit messages are `task-NNN: <title>`; commits use the parent's git identity. Claude Code does not push; the parent pushes (or says "push it").

## Screen, input and physics
- **D-013 / D-017** Logical height 720; logical width flexes 960 to 1920 (MAX_WIDTH 1920); gameplay-critical content fits the central 960x720 safe area; UI is anchored to screen edges/corners and respects device insets.
- **D-015** One input abstraction (actions `left`, `right`, `jump`, `power`). Touch buttons track each pointer continuously (a thumb can slide between buttons); mashing harmless. Keys: Left = ArrowLeft/A, Right = ArrowRight/D, Jump = Space/ArrowUp/W, Power = X/Z/Shift. Gamepad: d-pad or left stick, A = jump, X or B = power.
- **D-021** One shared physics file: run 200, walk 220 (ramp 60 ms up / 80 ms down), gravity 1800, jump velocity 760 (peak 2.5 tiles), coyote about 120 ms, jump buffer about 150 ms, fixed jump height.
- **D-029** Walking: hold = move at constant speed, release = stop, both held = stand, facing flips at once (hysteresis), soft level-end walls, no sliding.
- **D-045** Physics facts: a jump lasts 0.84 s and carries about 186 px running, 179-189 px from standing; 1-tile gap needs 44 px, 2-tile 108 px (71 px margin), 3-tile 172 px (7 px margin, too tight): gaps are 1 or 2 tiles only. Body 40 px wide. At least 7 tiles of ground between gaps (8 in Grass 5).
- **D-024 / D-030** "Watch me" demo: `controls.demo` is `false`, a tile form `{ tiles, jumpsAtTiles }` (auto-run) or a script form `{ script: [{ t, action: left|right|jump|power, hold? }] }`; a ghost presses through the same Player code; plays once per session per level; tap skips after 1.5 s; `?demo=0|1`.
- **D-028** Motion numbers live in `ANIM` (`core/levels/animation.ts`).

## Shooting, bubbles and the later levels
- **D-041** Grass 4 "Shoot" (Left, Right, Power only): a soft energy ball in the accent colour, horizontal from the muzzle in the facing direction; press fires, holding repeats; cooldown about 300 ms, at most 3 in flight, range about 640 px, speed about 700 px/s; never hurts anything. Friendly bubbles, no faces: small `o` (1 hit, 3 coins), big `O` (2x2 soft blocker until popped, 6 coins); hit area about 1.3x the picture; coins fly to the robot automatically; no failure, bubbles never move.
- **D-042** Small bubbles sit half a tile below the cell centre (shots leave 26-78 px above the feet); a Power press during the cooldown is remembered 0.3 s; shot hit size 14 px.
- **D-046** Grass 6 "Everything": Left, Right bottom-left; Jump (larger, 0.26 of the short edge, corner) and Power (0.22) beside it, at least 26 px apart; `?buttons=power` swaps the corner; pure `core/ui/buttonLayout.ts`; `h` high bubbles (4 coins, 2.75 tiles up, only hit from a jump peak; bonus); Jump and Power pulses take turns. Accepted: sliding a thumb across Power fires one harmless shot; bottom-row bubbles near the edge can sit behind buttons.
- **D-043** Order of the ladder: 008 Grass 5, 009 Grass 6, 010 parent view (all done).

## Progress storage, settings and parent view
- **D-036** Per-universe record `{ version: 1, chosenCharacterId (a colour id), look, levels: { [id]: { stars, bestCoins } }, settings }` in IndexedDB, then localStorage, then in memory (no dependency); failures never crash; an unknown version is played in memory and never overwritten.
- **D-047** Parent view: a grey gear on the map held 3 s (or `P` held 3 s, `?parent=1`); not reachable from levels, picker or win screen. Rows: sound on/off, button size (0.8/1.0/1.2/1.4 with a live preview of the real layout), main button (Jump/Power), look (robot/ball), unlock all, show demos again, Reset progress (held 3 s; keeps colour, look, settings), walk assist in Practice (D-049). Precedence everywhere: URL parameter, then saved setting, then default (`core/settings/settings.ts`; `?sound=`, `?buttonScale=`, `?buttons=`, `?look=`, `?unlock=all`, `?assist=`).

## Combo Practice (D-049)
Always open from a fixed teal button on the map; a selector with four icon cards (walk + shoot, jump + shoot, walk + jump, everything). Each run is a generated level (seeded, `core/levels/practice.ts`, about 120 tiles, D-045 rules, ends at the flag; Replay = new seed). A combo = an action button pressed while a nav button is held (120 ms grace), or Power in the air; minimum 400 ms between counted combos; each combo sparkles on the two buttons and fills one of five stars; five give a small celebration; no numbers, no failure, nothing saved. Ghost-hand coach after about 8 s without a combo (2.5 s, then quiet 20 s). Walk assist (parent setting, practice only, default Off): a quick tap latches walking, tap again stops. `?practice=<mode>|1`, `?seed=`.

## D-052 (2026-10-27) Browser fullscreen
In a normal browser tab the first real tap (same gesture family as the audio unlock) requests fullscreen and a landscape lock, once per page load, if the setting is on (default on), the browser supports it and the app is not installed. Never re-requested after the child leaves fullscreen; re-entry only through the parent view's Full screen row (the only control; hidden when unsupported or installed). `?fullscreen=0|1` > saved > default (D-047). A refusal or missing API is silent and never blocks play. Code lives in `core/ui/fullscreen.ts` (not `shell/`) so the universe's parent-view hooks may import it.

## D-053 (2026-10-28) Replay variety (Task 012; the task file says D-052, which Task 013 already took)
Parent answers: variety starts after the first clear; the rainbow bubble is in; Grass 1-2 vary but keep coins near demo jump columns fixed.
First play of a level is always the grid as written; any later play and the win screen's Replay get a shuffled grid with a fresh in-memory seed (nothing saved, progress and unlock rules unchanged). A level whose demo is about to play is always canonical. Only loose coins (as rigid clusters) and small bubbles move, sideways within their ground stretch and on their side of the start; rows, gaps, `S`, `F`, `#`, big and high bubbles, coins within 2 columns of a gap (arcs) and, in Grass 1-2, coins within 3 columns of a demo jump never move. Moved items keep 3 columns from gaps (3, or 2 if there is no other place, from start and flag), no coin touches a bubble or another coin, bubbles keep 1 tile between them; counts and kinds are preserved so star thresholds hold. `"variety": false` in a level's JSON opts out. Rainbow bubble (`r`, never in the JSON): 1 in 3 varied runs of levels with 4+ small bubbles (Grass 4, 6) turn one `o` into a rainbow bubble: 8 coins, rising three-note `rainbow` chime, colour rings; the extra coins only help. `?variety=0|1`, `?seed=`. Code: `core/levels/variety.ts`.

## D-055 (2026-10-28) The stage end is a robot charging pad, not a flag
Parent feedback: people who see him reach the flag ask "is it Mario?". Decision: replace the flag with an original, robot-themed end marker, the same on every level and in Practice: a **charging pad**, a wide glowing pad with a short post and a blinking beacon. On arrival the robot is eased onto the pad centre, the pad lights up (expanding rings, a beam of light, sparkles), the robot does its win pose, then the usual confetti, chime (the option's win chime) and result screen. Not chosen: rocket, rainbow gate, treasure chest (kept as ideas), random or per-world markers.
Data stays as it is: the grid letter `F` still means the goal (renaming the legend would touch every level file for no gain); only the drawing and the internal names change. Same trigger rules, physics, camera, demo and coach behaviour. Also in this task: the rainbow-bubble visibility fix from D-054, and an optional robot-themed coin face (the coin with a vertical slot also reads as Mario; the parent may veto it before the task is started).
